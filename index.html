World Models for Spatial Intelligence: From Policy Learning to Data Generation

World Models Spatial Intelligence Policy Learning Data Generation
Abstract & speaker bio+

Abstract. World models learn how the world evolves, providing an essential foundation for intelligent agents to understand, predict, and interact with their environments. In the context of spatial intelligence, world models can serve both as world representation models that facilitate policy learning for autonomous driving and embodied intelligence, and as world generators that support scene reconstruction, training data generation, and closed-loop simulation. This talk will present some explorations along these two directions. First, from the perspective of policy learning, this talk will discuss how latent-space world modeling, foundation models for video generation, and future spatial prediction can enhance spatiotemporal understanding, dynamics forecasting, and decision-making in autonomous driving and robotic manipulation. Second, from the perspective of data and scene generation, this talk will introduce some recent research on long-horizon interactive video generation and spatiotemporal memory modeling. Through these studies, this talk will illustrate how world models can bridge policy learning and generation, and discuss their future potential for advancing spatial intelligence.

Bio. 蒋理，香港中文大学（深圳）数据科学学院助理教授，校长青年学者，于深圳河套学院双聘。于2021年获得香港中文大学博士学位；其后在马克斯·普朗克研究所担任博士后研究员。其研究聚焦于计算机视觉与人工智能，具体方向包括三维感知与空间智能、世界模型、自动驾驶、具身智能、表征学习以及多模态学习等。其研究成果发表在CVPR、ICCV、NeurIPS、和TPAMI等顶级会议和期刊中，并多次获选口头报告与亮点论文，谷歌学术引用量超过18,000次，并担任顶会审稿人与领域主席。其关于自动驾驶运动预测的研究连续三次获得CVPR Waymo Open Dataset Motion Prediction Challenge全球冠军。多次入选由斯坦福大学与爱思唯尔联合发布的全球前2%顶尖科学家年度影响力榜单，并获得国家级青年人才项目资助。

Guangcong Wang

Assistant Professor, Great Bay University

Sep 9, 2026 11:00–11:50 Lecture Hall B

Towards Scalable Generalizable 3D Reconstruction in the Wild

Abstract & speaker bio+

Abstract. Recent advances in 3D Gaussian Splatting have enabled high-quality, real-time novel view synthesis, yet practical 3D reconstruction still faces several challenges: expensive per-scene optimization, dependence on calibrated cameras, and limited scalability to long image sequences. In this talk, I will present our recent efforts toward fast, generalizable, and scalable 3D reconstruction from real-world image collections. We first introduce MVSGaussian, which combines multi-view stereo with Gaussian representations to enable feed-forward reconstruction of unseen scenes, while retaining efficient rendering and fast scene-specific adaptation. We then move toward more unconstrained inputs with StructSplat, a feed-forward 3D Gaussian reconstruction framework that operates directly on uncalibrated sparse images. By explicitly organizing geometric, semantic, and texture information and jointly handling camera alignment, StructSplat improves both reconstruction fidelity and cross-scene generalization. Finally, we extend the problem from sparse, short-sequence reconstruction to long-sequence novel view synthesis through LongNVS-Bench, a large-scale benchmark containing 845 scenes and 5.62 million posed images. The benchmark exposes new challenges in long-range geometric consistency, repeated revisits, pose robustness, and computational scalability. Together, these works explore how generalizable 3D reconstruction can evolve from calibrated multi-view inputs to uncalibrated observations, and ultimately toward large-scale, long-sequence real-world environments.

Bio. 王广聪现任大湾区大学计算机与信息技术助理教授，并主导 Vision, Graphics, and X（VGX）研究组。研究兴趣主要包括三维视觉与具身智能。加入大湾区大学之前，曾在南洋理工大学 MMLab@NTU 担任研究员。于 2020 年获得中山大学博士学位。曾担任 CVPR、NeurIPS、WACV 和 BMVC 的领域主席，以及 AAAI 高级程序委员会委员。曾获评 ICCV 2023 杰出审稿人。
